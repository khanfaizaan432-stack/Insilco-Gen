# v0.34 JARVIS Synthesis, Critics, and Report Studio

v0.34 adds one bounded aggregate workspace over the controlled structured artifacts produced by earlier clinical milestones. It does not bypass intake, normalization, evidence-ledger, specialist-output, candidate-evidence, or human-review gates.

The JARVIS briefing is a deterministic case-state explanation, not a general assistant. It reports current state, missing information, readiness, proposed strategy state, reviewed evidence, unresolved conflicts, specialist disagreements, pending human decisions, limitations, and next workflow checkpoints. It cannot browse, spawn agents, modify evidence, approve conclusions, diagnose, prescribe, order tests, or classify variants.

Scientific synthesis claims retain stable claim identifiers, claim and origin categories, support states, uncertainty wording, and separate supporting, contradicting, and unresolved evidence links. A deterministic lifecycle policy classifies every ledger input as eligible, context-only, quarantined, or excluded. Withdrawn, retracted, invalid, and superseded records cannot support factual narrative; corrected and stale records remain explicit context only. Typed decisions and reason codes are serialized with provider, zero external-budget, fallback, role-mapping, and checksum context. User-supplied proposed claims remain excluded from factual report conclusions until they can be represented through controlled reviewed artifacts.

The fixed critic suite covers citation support, scientific consistency, evidence conflict, safety language, privacy, and provenance. Critics are non-mutating: they identify issues and propose corrections but do not rewrite source artifacts, settle disputes, approve conclusions, or fabricate citations.

Report Studio produces 19 distinct typed sections, including separate missing-information, readiness, disagreement, limitation, JARVIS-briefing, and review-history sections. Narratives expose claim ID, support, uncertainty, origin, evidence roles, conflict, review state, and draft state. Every narrative remains `draft_not_clinically_approved`. Human reviewers may accept, edit, reject, defer, or request more information through complete typed before/after snapshots. An explicit transition matrix makes accepted, edited, rejected, and deferred states terminal for ordinary replay. Stale, repeated-terminal, incomplete, unsupported, unsafe, or privacy-violating actions are rejected atomically with `validated_after=null`.

The established service bundle widths 5, 6, 7, and 8 remain unchanged. `build_clinical_case_v034_bundle` adds the aggregate as item nine. Stored runs, generated reports, the API, Workbench, provenance index, runtime lock, checksums, and the CLI expose the new workspace additively. Mock/deterministic behavior remains the default; no external LLM or external tool execution is introduced.
